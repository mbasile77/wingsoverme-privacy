Wings Over Me – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12/11/2026

Wings Over Me is an Android app that shows the aircraft flying near you. It is published by Marco Basile ("the developer"). Contact: wingsoverme.app@outlook.com.

Summary

The app has no accounts, no ads and no analytics. The developer does not collect, receive or store any personal data. To work, the app sends your position to a third-party flight-tracking service, as described below.

Location

With your permission, the app reads your device's precise location, only while the app is open on screen (never in the background). It is used to calculate which aircraft are near you and how far away they are.

To fetch nearby aircraft, the app sends your coordinates (rounded to four decimal places, about 10 metres) and the selected range directly from your device to the community flight-tracking service adsb.lol.

These requests contain no name, account or device identifier, but the service necessarily sees your IP address and may log requests under its own privacy policy. You can refuse or revoke the location permission at any time in Android settings; the app cannot show nearby aircraft without it.

Other network requests

  • adsbdb.com, hexdb.io – receive an aircraft's identifier or callsign to return its airline and route.
  • planespotters.net – receives an aircraft's identifier to return a photo.

These services also see your IP address and apply their own privacy policies. The radar map is stored inside the app and downloads nothing.

Data stored on your device

Your collection of spotted aircraft types, and your settings are stored only on your device. They are excluded from Android backup, are never uploaded, and are deleted when you clear the app's data or uninstall it.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal data from anyone.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no personal data about you, there is nothing to access, correct or delete on the developer's side. For data held by the third-party services listed above, please contact them directly. If you are in the EU/EEA you may also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a new date.
